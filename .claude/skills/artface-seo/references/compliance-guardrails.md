# Medical-Advertising Compliance Guardrails (CFM / ANVISA)

**Purpose:** keep every public, findable ArtFace content compliant with Brazilian medical-advertising norms. This is **principle-based guidance, not legal advice** — the practice's compliance/legal must confirm specifics (exact CFM resolutions/Manual de Publicidade Médica provisions and any ANVISA product rules evolve). When in doubt, route to legal and default to the more conservative option.

## Why this lives inside the SEO skill
The most **citable and rankable** health content is also the most **compliant**: factual, educational, physician-authored, non-sensational. Compliance and discoverability point the same direction — so we bake the guardrails in rather than bolting them on.

## Hard "avoid" list (do not publish)
- **Guaranteed/assured results** — "resultado garantido", "sem risco", "100% seguro", "definitivo".
- **Superlatives / self-promotion of superiority** — "o melhor", "número 1", "referência absoluta".
- **Sensationalism** — dramatizing, exploiting insecurity/self-esteem, "antes que seja tarde".
- **Before/after as promotional bait** — restricted; do not use to attract patients. Prefer education. If ever used, only within allowed contexts and with proper disclaimers — confirm with legal.
- **Promising specific outcomes for an individual** without the "results vary / requires medical evaluation" framing.
- **Price/discount promotions that trivialize** medical procedures; "compre já" urgency on health decisions.
- **Patient data exposure** — never reveal identifiable health info (incl. in review replies).
- **Equipment/technique claims** beyond approved indications; no unregistered product claims (ANVISA).

## Safe "do" list (compliant AND discoverable)
- **Educational, factual** procedure explanations (what it is, mechanism, indication, recovery, that it requires medical evaluation).
- **Real E-E-A-T**: physician name, **CRM** (and **RQE** where applicable), credentials, cited scientific references.
- **"Results vary; requires individual medical evaluation"** framing on any outcome discussion.
- **FAQ** answering genuine patient questions factually.
- **Clear identification** of the responsible physician and clinic.
- **Compliant titles/meta**: descriptive and factual, no superlatives (e.g. "Preenchimento de sulco nasogeniano — o que é e como funciona | ArtFace").

## Per-recommendation compliance note
Every content item the skill outputs carries a one-line note, e.g.:
- ✅ "Compliant — educational, no outcome promise."
- ⚠️ "Compliant only if before/after removed and 'resultados variam' added."
- ⛔ "Non-compliant as written (superlative) — replaced with factual title."

## Route-to-legal checklist (flag these, don't decide alone)
- Any use of before/after imagery.
- Any testimonial/review usage in ads.
- Any specific outcome, statistic, or comparative claim.
- Any product/equipment brand or efficacy claim.
- Promotions, pricing, or urgency mechanics on procedures.
- New procedure pages before first publication.

> Default posture: **educational, factual, physician-authored, conservative**. It protects the practice and, not by coincidence, is exactly what ranks and gets cited by AI.
