# Dental-Advertising Compliance Guardrails (CFO / ANVISA)

**Purpose:** keep every public, findable ArtFace content compliant with Brazilian **dental**-advertising norms. ArtFace's responsible professional, **Dr. Rafael Lucci dos Santos, is a cirurgião-dentista (CRO-SC 18.965 / CRO-PR 17.398), especialista em Harmonização Orofacial** — **not** a physician. The governing body is the **CFO (Conselho Federal de Odontologia)**, **not** the CFM. This is **principle-based guidance, not legal advice** — the practice's compliance/legal must confirm specifics (CFO resolutions and ANVISA product rules evolve). When in doubt, route to legal and default to the more conservative option.

## Why CFO, not CFM — and why it matters
- Harmonização Orofacial (HOF) is a **recognized odontology specialty** (Res. CFO-198/2019). The E-E-A-T anchor is **"Cirurgião-dentista, especialista em Harmonização Orofacial — CRO-SC 18.965"**.
- **Never** publish "médico", "CRM", or "RQE" — that would be a **false professional claim**. Use **CRO** and "cirurgião-dentista".
- **Key differentiator:** under **Res. CFO-196/2019**, **before/after ("antes e depois") is PERMITTED for dentists** (results of diagnosis/conclusion) — where CFM heavily restricts it for physicians. So ArtFace has a *compliant* before/after lever many physician-run competitors lack — **if** used strictly to the CFO rules below and cleared by legal.

## Why this lives inside the SEO skill
The most **citable and rankable** health content is also the most **compliant**: factual, educational, professional-authored, non-sensational. Compliance and discoverability point the same direction — bake the guardrails in.

## Hard "avoid" list (do not publish)
- **False title** — never "médico/CRM/RQE"; ArtFace is dentistry (CRO).
- **Guaranteed/assured results** — "resultado garantido", "sem risco", "100% seguro", "definitivo".
- **Superlatives / claims of superiority** — "o melhor", "número 1", "referência absoluta". ("premium" as a brand descriptor is tolerable — monitor.)
- **Sensationalism** — dramatizing, exploiting insecurity/self-esteem, "antes que seja tarde".
- **"Durante" imagery** — mid-procedure/surgical imagery is prohibited (Art. 44, Código de Ética Odontológica), even though before/after is allowed.
- **Before/after misuse** — permitted only per CFO-196/2019: documented patient consent, no "durante", no guarantee, not sensational bait. Legal signs off the exact usage + disclaimer.
- **Promising individual outcomes** without "resultados variam / exige avaliação individual".
- **Price/discount sensationalism or urgency** ("compre já") on procedures.
- **Patient data exposure** — never reveal identifiable health info (incl. in review/ReclameAqui replies).
- **Product/equipment overclaim** — stay within ANVISA-approved indications; no unregistered-product claims.

## Safe "do" list (compliant AND discoverable)
- **Correct identity:** "Cirurgião-dentista, especialista em Harmonização Orofacial — **CRO-SC 18.965**".
- **Educational, factual** procedure explanations (what it is, mechanism, indication, recovery, "exige avaliação individual").
- **Real E-E-A-T**: name, **CRO**, HOF specialty (Res. CFO-198/2019), formação, cited references. Never fabricate.
- **"Resultados variam; exige avaliação individual"** on any outcome discussion.
- **FAQ** answering genuine patient questions factually.
- **Compliant before/after** (per CFO-196/2019, legal-approved) as an *educational* differentiator — never as the lead sensational bait.
- **Compliant titles/meta**: descriptive/factual, no superlatives (e.g. "Bigode chinês (sulco nasogeniano) em Balneário Camboriú — o que é e como funciona | ArtFace").
- **Correct schema types:** `Dentist` / `MedicalClinic` (not `Physician`).

## Per-recommendation compliance note
Every content item carries a one-line note:
- ✅ "Compliant — educational, no outcome promise."
- ⚠️ "Compliant only per CFO-196 (consent, no 'durante', disclaimer) — legal sign-off."
- ⛔ "Non-compliant as written (superlative / says 'médico') — replaced."

## Route-to-legal checklist (flag; don't decide alone)
- Confirm the HOF specialty registration (Res. CFO-198/2019).
- Any before/after usage (CFO-196/2019 conditions + disclaimer).
- Any testimonial/review reused in ads (consent + CFO review).
- Any specific outcome, statistic, or comparative claim.
- Any product/equipment brand or efficacy claim (ANVISA).
- Promotions, pricing, or urgency mechanics on procedures.
- ReclameAqui / review replies (no patient data; no public clinical dispute).
- New procedure page before first publication.

> Default posture: **educational, factual, dentist-authored (CRO), conservative** — simultaneously the most CFO-compliant and the most rankable/citable content.
