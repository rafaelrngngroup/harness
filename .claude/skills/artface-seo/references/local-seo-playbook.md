# Local SEO Playbook — Facial-Aesthetics Clinic

For a clinic, **local signals usually outrank generic organic**: a patient searching "harmonização facial [cidade]" or "clínica de estética facial perto de mim" gets a Maps pack + reviews before blue links. This is the highest-leverage domain for ArtFace. Do it first.

## 1. Google Business Profile (GBP) — the anchor
Score each: Have / Partial / Missing / Unknown-provide-to-run.

- **Claimed & verified** profile for the physical clinic.
- **Primary category** precise (e.g. "Clínica de estética" / "Médico" / dermatology/plastic as applicable) + relevant secondary categories.
- **Name** = real clinic name only (no keyword stuffing — against GBP guidelines and risks suspension).
- **Address** exact + **service area** (cities served) if applicable.
- **Phone** (local number), **WhatsApp**, **website**, **booking link**.
- **Hours** accurate incl. holidays.
- **Services** list populated with the priority procedures (using compliant, factual names).
- **Photos**: clinic exterior/interior, team, equipment (respecting patient privacy & compliance — avoid before/after as bait).
- **Attributes**: accessibility, payment, "por agendamento", etc.
- **Q&A**: seed real FAQs; monitor and answer.
- **Posts**: periodic educational updates (compliant).

## 2. NAP consistency (Name / Address / Phone)
Inconsistent NAP fragments local trust and confuses both Maps and AI models.

- The **exact same** name, address, and phone must appear identically on: the site (footer + contact page), GBP, Instagram bio/contact, and any directory (Doctoralia, etc.).
- Add **`LocalBusiness`/`MedicalBusiness` Schema.org** on the site with the same NAP (see on-page domain).
- Audit for old addresses/phones on stale directories and fix them.

## 3. Reviews (compliant)
Reviews are a top local-ranking factor and a trust signal for patients and AI.

- Establish a **simple, ethical review-request flow** (e.g. a post-visit link) — never incentivized/paid reviews (against platform policy and medical ethics).
- **Respond** to reviews professionally, **without disclosing any patient health information** (privacy/ethics) — thank generically, take specifics offline.
- Aim for steady velocity over spikes.

## 4. Directories & citations
- **Doctoralia** and reputable health directories: complete, consistent profile (this is also a strong corroboration source for AI-search).
- Local business listings with consistent NAP.
- Avoid spammy directories.

## 5. Local landing pages (per city/region × per procedure)
When ArtFace serves more than one city or wants to rank for "[procedimento] em [cidade]":

- One clear page per priority procedure, with a **local section** (city, address, map embed) where genuine.
- Do **not** create thin duplicate pages for cities you don't genuinely serve (doorway pages are penalized and ethically dubious for a clinic).
- Each page: compliant title ("[Procedimento] em [Cidade] — ArtFace | Dr. Rafael, CRM …"), factual description, FAQ, `MedicalProcedure` + `FAQPage` schema, real NAP.
